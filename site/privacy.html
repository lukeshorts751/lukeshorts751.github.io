Privacy Policy – Luke_Shorts

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Luke_Shorts is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Luke Shorts YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload solely to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and privacy status on the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Luke_Shorts' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
